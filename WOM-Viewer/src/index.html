    WOM Viewer for Wurm Unlimited

    Views Wurm Unlimited .wom models straight from your own game folder: skinned characters and creatures, animations from properties.xml, item texture/material variants from mappings.txt, and player outfits, faces and hair layered the way the game does it from armor.xml and mappings.txt. Nothing is uploaded: files are read by your browser on your own computer. No game files are included with this viewer.

    For modding Wurm Unlimited (single player or player-hosted servers).

    Tips

    • Open game's .jar files: pick graphics.jar from <game>/packs. It is read while still zipped.
    • Open custom folder: your converted or edited files (e.g. player_obj). Its .obj/.wom models and textures replace the game's where the path matches and are marked (CF) in the list. Its mappings.txt or mappings_matched.txt is used on top of the game's.
    • Upscale folder: the output of upscale; its textures are the 4096 choice in the Textures menu.
    • Custom object: new or replacing objects. A new hair named like the game's (femaleHair08.obj) appears in the Hair menu.
    • Compare: the left side can be the game .jar or the custom folder, at its own texture size (e.g. .jar 2048 against custom + 4096).
    • Settings (outfit, hair, face, dyes, animation, hidden meshes) are kept for each object while you switch; Default at the top of the right panel resets them.
