1. Where things are

The staging folder holds the extracted originals, the upscaled textures and the finished packs (in its jars folder). A full run needs roughly 15–25 GB.

2. Upscaler

GPU number images on the GPU at once CPU workers
pictures smaller than × the same in pixels (icons) – dye masks and the sky/sun colour tables are always kept as they are; the gui and TutorialImages folders are never upscaled

Every texture is upscaled 2× (largest 4096×4096) and saved in its own format – .dds keeps its compression and mipmaps. Where a texture comes in 256/512/1024/2048 folders, each folder gets the real art of the one above it, and only the top one is AI-upscaled (2048 → 4096).

3. Packs

Press “Read mappings.txt” to see which packs will be made.

4. Run

Stopping (or a power cut) is safe: Start / Resume checks what is already finished – half-written textures, mappings.txt and .jar files are made again – and carries on. Copy the packs you want from the jars folder into WurmLauncher\userpacks.
